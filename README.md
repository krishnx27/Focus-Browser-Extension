# 🎯 FocusBoard — What It Is & How It Works

### 📌 The Real Problem:
Every time we open a **New Tab** to search for something or do some work, we get distracted out of habit. We instinctively click on social media shortcuts or start watching videos on YouTube. Before we know it, 30–40 minutes are wasted, and we forget the main priority we set for the day.

### 💡 The Solution:
**FocusBoard** replaces the cluttered, default Chrome New Tab with a clean, calm productivity dashboard. It gives you a peaceful starting point every time you open a tab and keeps your **#1 priority for the day** right in front of your eyes.

---

## 🚀 How to Use It (Your Daily Routine):

### 1. Start Your Day with One Main Focus
* When you open Chrome in the morning, you are greeted with a calm background, live clock, and a personal greeting (*"Good morning, Krishna"*).
* Right in the center, it asks you: **“What is your main focus today?”**
* Type your most important goal for the day (e.g., *"Finish the project presentation"* or *"Complete 3 coding problems"*) and hit Enter.
* For the rest of the day, every time you open a new tab, you will see this reminder. At midnight, it automatically resets so you can start the next day fresh.

### 2. Track Quick To-Dos on the Side
* On the right-hand side, there is a neat, transparent task panel.
* Add your small tasks for the day (e.g., *"Send email to team"*, *"Review notes"*).
* Once done, simply click on the task to mark it complete with a strikethrough. You can easily delete completed tasks with one click.

### 3. Turn on "Focus Mode" When Doing Serious Work
* Whenever you need to sit down for deep study or work, just click the **Focus Mode toggle** at the bottom-left corner. You’ll see a quick **“Locked In 🔒”** alert.
* Now, if you try opening **YouTube, Instagram, Reddit, or Twitter** out of habit:
  * The site **will not open**.
  * Instead, it instantly redirects you to a **“Take a Moment”** screen, reminding you of today’s main focus with a clean **“Go Back to Work”** button.

### 4. Personalize as Per Your Choice
* Click the **Settings (⚙️) icon** at the top-left:
  * **Your Name**: Change your name anytime to customize the greeting.
  * **Clock Style**: Easily switch between 12-hour (AM/PM) and 24-hour formats.
  * **Custom Wallpaper**: Upload your own favorite wallpaper photo (up to 5MB) or reset back to default.
  * **Blocked Sites**: Add your own custom websites (like `netflix.com`, `facebook.com`) to the block list.
